# 卷十二 五、郑莱论领袖

> 卷十二（1915–1916）　序号 793 / 1006　来源: [https://www.xuges.com/xdmj/hushi/lx3/086.htm](https://www.xuges.com/xdmj/hushi/lx3/086.htm)

---

（一月四日）

There are those who are destined to become leaders of men. They think hard and
work hard: that is the secret of leadership.

—Loy Chang

〔中译〕

有些人命中注定要成领袖。他们勤于思考，努力工作：此乃领导之秘诀所在。

——郑莱
