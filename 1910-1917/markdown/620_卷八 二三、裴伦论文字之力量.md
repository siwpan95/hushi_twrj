# 卷八 二三、裴伦论文字之力量

> 卷八（1914–1915）　序号 620 / 1006　来源: [https://www.xuges.com/xdmj/hushi/lx2/108.htm](https://www.xuges.com/xdmj/hushi/lx2/108.htm)

（二月）

But words are things, and a small drop of ink,

Falling like dew upon a thiught, produces

That which makes thousands, perhaps millions, think.

—Byron,Don Juan, Canto Ⅲ, St. 88.

〔中译〕

语言是看得见的东西，一滴墨水

像露珠滴在思想之上，

它使无数人展开了思维的翅膀。

——裴伦：《唐璜》
