# 卷九 七〇、英国哲学家鲍生葵之言

> 卷九（1915）　序号 699 / 1006　来源: [https://www.xuges.com/xdmj/hushi/lx2/187.htm](https://www.xuges.com/xdmj/hushi/lx2/187.htm)

（六月三日）

My present self was not born of my actual parents at such and such a date and
place. It was born when I met such a friend or was taught by such a teacher, or
was awakened by such an experience.

—B. Bosanquet:Gifford Lectures, Vol. Ⅱ, Ⅸ.

〔中译〕

当下之我，并非吾父母于某某时刻、某某地点所生之我，而是结识益友并受其影响之我，而是受良师诤诤教诲之我，而是受如此经历之启迪之我。

——B. 鲍生葵：《吉福德之演讲》，第二，九卷
