My dear Sir:
It was with great regret that I heard this morning
of the unfortunate circumstances attending your visit to
Mrs. Fritz last evening. I assure you that it was due
only to the over zealousness of some of the employees of
Cathey Mansions. Unfortunately only yesterday morn-
ing the Lift Boys had been especially warned about tak-
ing any one into the Lifts who might to soliciting busi-
ness or who might, in any way be annoying to the
Guests as it is our desire to protect our Guests from
these very insistent merchants. It is greatly to be de-
plored, however, that these Boys should not have rec-
ognized that you were coming as a Guest to the house.
They have all been reprimanded very seriously and I
earnestly trust ihat a happening of this character will
not occur again.
Wili you kindly accept our sincere apologies, both
for our Boys and also for any apparent negligence on our

亲爱的先生：
今天早晨，我听说了你昨晚访问弗里茨女士受到
拦阻的不幸事件，对此我深表歉意。我确信，这是中
国公寓大厦一些职员过于热心所致。凑巧的是，昨天
导晨，开电梯的人接到特别通知：要注意那些乘电梯
的人，他们有的来拉客做生意，有的来骚扰旅客。我
们这样做是想保护旅客，免受商人的骚扰。然而，遗
憾的是，我们的职员没有认出你，不知道你是我们的
贵宾。他们已经受到严厉的处罚。我诚挚地向你保
证：今后不会再发生此类事件。
我代表我们的职员，向你真诚道歉。由于我们的
疏忽给你造成的麻烦，请你原谅。
您的真诚的经理：M、洛根
