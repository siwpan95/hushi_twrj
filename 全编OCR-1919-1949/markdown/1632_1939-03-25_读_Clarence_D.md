One thing that especially impressed me was the ea-
gerhess with which all the religionists have seized upon
the cryptic,imaginary, & more or less valid assertions of
Eddington & Jeans. No one doubts their learning &
brilliancy in their special field, but their field is not reli-
gion. In that they stand like all of the rest of us, Their
claims carry no authority, but they are to be judged by
their investigation of matter outside their realm, and on
the consistency & reasonahleness of their opinions. (P.
383)(cf.P.399)[特别使我印象深刻的一件事是，所有
的宗教狂都急切地利用了那神秘的、虚构的并且或多
