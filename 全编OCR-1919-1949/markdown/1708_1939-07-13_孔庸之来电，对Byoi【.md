Yen May replace Hu Shih?
CHUNKING,China,July 12(UP).
reporis current here today said that Dr. W. W. Yen
would be made Chinese Ambassador at Washington, a
post he held frormn 1929 to 1932 , to succeed Dr. Hu Shih,
Reported to have been in ill health for months past. A
Foreign Minister Spokesman said, however, that Dr.
Shih would remain as Ambassador "for the present. "
【译文】颜可能取代胡适
【联合通讯社中国重庆7月12日电］据此间风
传的报道称，W.W.Yen【颜惠庆】博士将出任他曾
在一九二九年到一九三二年担任过的驻华盛顿中国大
使一职，取代据报道在过去几个月中一-直健康状况不
好的胡适博士。然而，外交部发言人说，胡适博士
“目前”仍将担任大使。
